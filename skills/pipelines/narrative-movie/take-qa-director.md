# Take QA Director — Narrative Movie Pipeline

Inspect actual generated clips and produce `take_qa_report` before picture editing.

Review the whole clip, with extra attention to the first and last frames:

- identity, wardrobe, props, and environment;
- blocking, eyelines, screen direction, and action order;
- intended versus unwanted camera motion;
- entrance and exit state;
- visual artifacts, audio defects, and intent mismatch;
- head/tail handles and usable in/out range.

Diagnose the first observable failure and choose a targeted action: trim, retry, revise prompt, strengthen a reference, switch the approved workflow contract, resplit the shot, or use the planned fallback. Preserve rejected takes and reasons. Human approval selects the usable take set.
